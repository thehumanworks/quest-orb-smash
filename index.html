How to play
  • Smash glowing orbs with your sabres (or bare hands: fingertips and palms).
  • Gold = +50, red spiky bombs = −40 and break your combo.
  • Chain hits for a combo multiplier up to ×8. Missing an orb aimed at you resets it.
  • Pull the trigger (or pinch) to ready up. Versus is best of 3 rounds.
  • Desktop: click orbs to smash, Space = ready.
